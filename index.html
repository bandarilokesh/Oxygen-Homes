Are Oxygen Homes projects approved by HMDA and TS-RERA?

Yes, completely. Both Oxygen City Triplex Villas (Mallampet) and Oxygen Pearl (Pragathi Nagar/Gajularamaram) have full statutory clearances from HMDA and TS-RERA (RERA ID: P02200003461), ensuring 100% legal security and bank loan availability.

What configurations and plot sizes are available at Oxygen City?

Oxygen City features 99 limited edition G+2 Triplex Luxury Villas on 168 Sq. Yards and 200 Sq. Yards plots, available in both East and West facing Vastu compliant designs with super built-up areas ranging between 3,100 Sft and 3,750 Sft.

Our projects are approved by leading nationalized and private financial institutions including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Bank of Baroda. Eligible buyers can avail up to 80% financing with expedited processing.
